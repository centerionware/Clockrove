## Using Clockrove

Clockrove helps you record work time, manage jobs and tasks, and prepare
reports and business documents. Use the app with data you are authorized to
handle, including customer details, work locations, and attachments.

Review work entries and generated documents before using them for billing,
payroll, or reporting. Android permissions, location accuracy, and background
processing affect when automated clock-ins and clock-outs occur. You can use
manual timekeeping and correct saved entries.

Keep backups of records you need. The app encrypts backups with the password
you choose. Clockrove cannot recover that password. Before restoring, review
the proposed changes and any conflicts.
