# Clockrove Privacy Policy

**Release draft.** Before publishing, confirm the developer identity shown in
Google Play, the effective date, the privacy contact, and the documentation
site's hosting and logging practices. Review the SDKs and network behavior of
the signed release candidate against these disclosures.

## What Clockrove uses

Clockrove keeps your jobs, timesheets, task boards, customers, invoices, quotes,
attachments, and preferences in app-private storage on your device. This
information supports the features you use. Clockrove has no Clockrove account
or synchronization service. The app has no advertising or analytics.

When you permit work location recording, Clockrove stores location samples,
timestamps, and accuracy during work sessions. When you enable site automation,
Android location services can monitor enabled sites in the background to
detect arrival and departure. Clockrove uses those transitions for timer
decisions. It does not retain an off-clock location trail. You can change
Android location permissions and use the manual timer without location access.

## When data goes outside Clockrove

Opening maps asks the configured map provider for tiles. The default provider
is OpenStreetMap. Tile requests expose the viewed map area and ordinary
connection information such as your IP address to that provider. If you
configure a custom tile service, it receives those requests and any
authentication header you configure for it.

When you save a backup or export, Android passes the file to the storage
location or provider you select. When you share a document, Android gives the
receiving app access to that document. The services you select handle those
copies under their own policies. Clockrove cannot remove copies held elsewhere.

## Protection, retention, and deletion

Clockrove keeps app data until you delete it or clear the app's local storage.
Features such as location history let you delete a selected range. Local
revision records can preserve change and deletion history. Android automatic
app backup is disabled for Clockrove. Clockrove encrypts its backup files with
the password you choose. Keep your password and copies secure, because
Clockrove cannot recover a forgotten backup password.
