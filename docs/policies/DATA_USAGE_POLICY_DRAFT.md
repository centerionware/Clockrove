# Clockrove Data Usage Policy

**Release draft.** This guide describes the controls in the Android app.
The [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md) describes handling and sharing.

## Choose what Clockrove records

- Use the manual timer to track work without granting location access.
- Change the default work location recording setting or override it for a job.
  This controls the location history that Clockrove saves during sessions.
- Enable site automation separately if you want arrival or departure detection.
  Android may require precise and background location permission. To stop this
  behavior, disable site automation or revoke its location permission.
- GPS recording and site automation serve different purposes. Turning off
  work location recording still allows enabled sites to monitor transitions.

## Review, export, or delete

- Work history and reports show the records retained on your device. Use the
  location deletion option to remove GPS points without deleting timesheets.
- CSV and PDF exports may contain private business or location details. Review
  what you are exporting before selecting a destination or sharing app.
- Use an encrypted backup to transfer your data. Keep the password separately.
  Restore lets you review a proposed merge and requires confirmation before
  applying it. Imported site automation starts disabled.
- Clearing Clockrove's app storage removes local app records. Save a backup
  first if you intend to keep them. Files already exported or shared are
  controlled by their destination.
