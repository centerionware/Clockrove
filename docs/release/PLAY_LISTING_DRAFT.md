## App name

Clockrove

## Short description

Track work time, organize jobs, and keep your records on your device.

## Full description

Clockrove helps independent workers keep track of time and manage work by
job. Start and stop a timer, add missed entries, and review your work history
before billing or reporting.

- Organize job tasks on boards and reuse saved tasks.
- Review time and totals across a date range and export reports as CSV.
- Prepare quotes and invoices, save PDF copies, and record payments.
- Use the Android home-screen widget to start or stop work.
- Enable work-site automation to start or stop the timer when Android detects
  a site transition. Delivery depends on location permissions, accuracy and
  Android background behavior. Manual timing is always available.
- Save a password-encrypted backup and review changes before merging a
  backup into your existing records.

Clockrove stores work records on your device and needs no Clockrove account.
Online maps use the configured map provider. You choose where to save
exports and backups or which apps receive shared documents.
