Clockrove Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Applies to the Clockrove Android app and this website.

Summary

Clockrove is a local-first timekeeping app. Your jobs, work records, tasks, customer details, documents and preferences are stored on your own device. Clockrove has no account system, no server and no synchronization service, and the developer does not receive your records.

Clockrove contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. It does not sell personal information and does not share it with data brokers.

Information Clockrove stores on your device

  • Work entries, timers, job and task records, notes, photos and attachments you add.
  • Business details you enter, such as customers, rates, quotes, invoices and payments.
  • Work locations you save, and location samples recorded during work when you enable that setting.
  • App preferences, including your chosen language and map settings.
  • Local change history used for records, reports and backup merge review.

This information stays in Clockrove's private app storage unless you export, back up or share it.

Location

Location is optional. Manual timekeeping works with no location permission.

  • If you enable work location recording, Clockrove saves location samples, timestamps and accuracy while a work session is running. It is not a continuous location trail and it is not recorded off the clock.
  • If you enable site automation, Android and Google Play services monitor the work sites you define so Clockrove can detect arrival and departure. Clockrove stores only the resulting membership facts and its own timer decisions. Location monitoring stops if you disable automation or revoke the permission.
  • Clockrove asks for precise location and, where Android requires it, background location only for the site automation you turn on.

What can leave your device

  • Maps. Opening a map asks the map provider for map tiles. The default provider is OpenStreetMap, which receives the viewed map area, zoom level and ordinary connection data such as your IP address. A custom tile service you configure receives those requests and any header you configure for it. If you search for an address in a work-site map, the text you submit is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service; this lookup needs no account and does not depend on which tile provider you configured.
  • Your exports. When you save a backup, export CSV or PDF, or share a document, Android passes the file to the location, storage provider or app you choose. Those copies are governed by the destination's own policies.
  • Android services. Clockrove uses Android and Google Play services for location, geofencing, notifications, file access and purchases. Those components process data on your device under Google's terms.
  • This website. This site is static. It uses no cookies, accounts or analytics. The hosting provider may keep ordinary web-server logs for security and delivery.

Apart from those user-directed or platform flows, Clockrove does not send your records anywhere, and the developer cannot access them.

Your choices and controls

  • Turn work location recording off by default, or for an individual job.
  • Turn site automation off, or remove a work site, at any time.
  • Change or revoke location permission in Android settings.
  • Delete saved location ranges without deleting your timesheets.
  • Export an encrypted backup, then clear the app's storage to remove local records.

Files you already exported or shared are controlled by their destination, and Clockrove cannot delete them.

Retention and security

Clockrove keeps records until you delete them or clear the app's storage. The app does not use Android automatic cloud backup. Backups you create are encrypted with the password you choose; keep that password separately, because Clockrove cannot recover it. Your device's own lock screen and encryption are the main protection for the app's data.

Children

Clockrove is a business timekeeping tool, not a service directed to children, and it is not intended for use by children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes that affect what leaves your device will be described in the app's release notes.
