Clockrove Data Use Policy

Effective 24 November 2026. This page summarizes the same commitments as the Privacy Policy, organized around the controls in the app.

Our commitment

Clockrove processes your information on your device. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your work records, customer details, documents or location history. There is no Clockrove account, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Work records: timers, entries, jobs, tasks, notes, photos and attachments. Stored in private app storage on your device.
  • Business records: customers, rates, quotes, invoices and payments you enter. Stored on your device.
  • Location history: samples taken only while a work session runs and only when you enable recording. Stored on your device; never uploaded to the developer.
  • Site automation: the work sites you define and the arrival/departure facts Android reports to the app. Stored on your device; processed with Google Play services location on the device.
  • Preferences: language, theme, map and timer settings. Stored on your device.

When information leaves the device

Only in these situations, and always because you or Android initiated it:

  • You open a map, so the map provider receives tile requests and connection data. The default is OpenStreetMap; a custom provider you configure receives the same. If you search for an address in a work-site map, the text you submit goes to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service; no account is needed.
  • You export, back up or share a file, so the destination you choose receives it.
  • Android or Google Play services handles location, notifications, file access or purchases on your device.

Controls you have

  • Record work location by default, per job, or not at all.
  • Enable site automation per job, and remove sites you no longer use.
  • Delete saved location ranges while keeping timesheets.
  • Create an encrypted backup and then clear the app's storage.
  • Revoke location or notification permission in Android settings. Manual timekeeping keeps working.

Google Play data safety

Clockrove's Google Play data safety declaration is completed from this policy. Your work records, customer details and location history are not transmitted to the developer, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The Privacy Policy lists every flow where data leaves the device at your request or through Android and Google Play services, so the store declaration and the policy can be checked against each other.
